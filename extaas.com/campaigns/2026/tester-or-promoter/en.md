# Get free access: become an Extaas tester or promoter

Would you like to use an Extaas product, help improve its experience or introduce it to your audience? Apply as a tester or promoter. Approved participants receive the selected licence or service **free for an initial 1–2 months**, with a possible extension after a useful contribution.

Choose the path that fits you:

- **Tester** — use the product in normal conditions, identify friction and provide clear feedback.
- **Promoter** — create an original video or another substantive presentation based on genuine product use.

The programme covers products in the Extaas ecosystem. Access is not automatic: name the product you want when applying and wait for our written approval.

## What does a tester receive, and what do we expect?

A tester receives a free period for the agreed product and an opportunity to influence its user experience through practical feedback. We are looking for more than “I like it” or “I do not like it”: the feedback should help us understand and improve something concrete.

Your feedback must include at least:

- what you tested and in which environment;
- reproducible steps for any issue found;
- the expected and actual result;
- observations about usability, reliability and documentation;
- a short results summary at the end of the agreed period.

The tester path may suit you if you use digital products carefully, can describe problems clearly and follow through on agreements. Previous testing experience is useful, but a thoughtful test plan matters more when applying.

## What does a promoter receive, and what do we expect?

A promoter receives free access to the agreed product and creates an original, useful introduction for their audience. This can be a video or another format agreed before work begins.

To apply as a promoter, your channel or account must have an audience of at least **10,000 followers or viewers**. Before content creation starts, we agree in writing on the product, core requirements, format, publishing channel and proof of publication.

The content must reflect genuine product use. The collaboration and free access must be disclosed clearly in accordance with the platform's rules and applicable advertising requirements.

## How long is the free period?

An approved tester or promoter receives the selected licence or service free for an initial **1–2 months**.

- A tester may receive an extension after submitting the required feedback.
- A promoter may receive an extension after publishing and submitting the agreed content.
- The extension depends on the quality, scope and usefulness of the contribution and may range from a few months up to two years.

This is not paid work or monetary sponsorship. Extensions are neither automatic nor guaranteed; each one is confirmed separately.

## Which types of testing require separate permission?

Normal use and evaluation of the agreed features are covered by the programme. Security testing, load testing, bypassing restrictions or attempting exploits against web services, accounts, APIs or other online systems is not normal use.

Such testing always requires our prior written permission, a defined scope and explicit rules. Participation in the tester programme alone does not provide permission. Home Assistant applications may be evaluated in normal use without separate permission, but this does not authorise attacks against online services they depend on.

## Usage limits for Home Assistant applications

Our Home Assistant applications are intended primarily for home and personal use. If you use them for business or in a business-critical environment, you do so at your own risk. They have not undergone extensive high-load testing, and we do not have a complete picture of their behaviour under prolonged or heavy load.

Do not use them as the sole control or safety layer where failure could cause significant harm.

## How do I apply?

[Send us your application](https://oux.ee/@/contact?popover=contact_popover) and include:

1. whether you are applying as a tester or promoter;
2. which Extaas product you want to use;
3. how you intend to test or present it;
4. for promoters, a link to your channel or account and its audience size;
5. for testers, a short description of your test environment and relevant experience.

We review each application. If it is a fit, we will send you the access details, free-period terms and concrete next steps in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does every applicant receive free access?

No. Free access begins only after the application is approved and confirmed by us in writing.

### Does a tester need a large audience?

No. The audience requirement of at least 10,000 applies to promoters. For testers, we focus on the test plan, environment and ability to provide useful feedback.

### Is a promoter paid?

No. The programme is not paid work or monetary sponsorship. The value of an approved collaboration is free use of the agreed licence or service for the confirmed period.

### Is two years of free use guaranteed?

No. The initial period is 1–2 months. An extension may reach up to two years depending on the contribution, but it is decided separately and is not guaranteed.

### May I perform security or load testing?

Only if you receive our written permission, the agreed scope and the testing rules before you begin.
