# OUX builds; Extaas puts solutions to work

OUX and Extaas have distinct roles in one ecosystem. **OUX is the development side**, where requirements, technical decisions and custom work begin. **Extaas is the home of finished products**, where public tools, services and business workflows become available to use.

## Why separate them?

The division keeps development services and ready-made products understandable. A customer does not need to know which repository or service powers a solution: Extaas presents the product, its terms and the next step. When a ready-made option is not enough, OUX can assess a practical customisation or new build.

## Practical value

- one starting point for ready-to-use digital solutions;
- tools that can be connected as needs grow;
- a clear path to custom development when standard functionality is insufficient;
- decisions grounded in maintainability, security and proven capability.
